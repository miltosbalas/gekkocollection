The Villa

A Sense of Place

Casa Gekko is conceived as a space of quiet sophistication, where clean architectural lines, natural textures and abundant light come together effortlessly. Open living areas extend into expansive outdoor spaces, creating a seamless connection between the interior and the surrounding landscape — always centered around the endless blue of the sea.

Located in one of Mykonos' most sought-after areas, Casa Gekko offers a rare balance of privacy and accessibility — surrounded by natural beauty, yet just a short drive from Mykonos Town, its beaches and its nightlife.
