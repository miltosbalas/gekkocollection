The Villa

Tranquility, Privacy & the Cycladic Light

Located in the peaceful area of Ornos, only a five-minute walk from Ornos and Korfos beaches and a short ride from cosmopolitan Mykonos Town, Grace Villa is a 138 sq.m. three-level property — part of an exclusive complex of five villas — with whitewashed Cycladic architecture, wooden pergolas and sweeping sea views.

The villa accommodates up to eleven guests across five bedrooms, with a Master Suite offering a private balcony and panoramic veranda overlooking the Aegean. Guests enjoy a large shared pool, alfresco dining terraces and complete tranquility, minutes from the island's nightlife.
